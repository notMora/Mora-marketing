## Proof rules (non-negotiable)
- There are **no real client results yet**. Portfolio = "concept projects". Never invent clients, testimonials, numbers, reviews or "we helped X get Y".
- Allowed proof: concept redesigns (label "concept"), process transparency, live demos, Jordi's expertise, public stats **with a named source**.
- Never name or mock a real local business without permission; use "a salon in Zürich" style anonymised examples.
- Physio content: no health claims, no patient data.

## Voice
- First person singular ("I build…", "I'll show you…"). Direct, calm, confident, zero jargon. Swiss precision, not hype.
- Short sentences. Concrete numbers of time/effort ("3 taps", "at 11pm", "in 20 minutes").
- Talks to the business owner, not to designers. Sells outcomes (more bookings, fewer DMs, time back), never features for their own sake.
- Banned: "elevate", "unlock", "game-changer", "in today's digital world", emoji walls, fake urgency, ALL-CAPS sentences in captions.

## Visual system (carousels & covers)
- Palette: black `#0A0A0A` · lime accent `#C6FF3D` · off-white `#FAFAFA` · dark card `#141414` · border `#262626` · grey text `#8E8E8E` / `#C6C6C7`.
- Type: **Inter** (400–800). Headlines uppercase, tight tracking (-0.04em), huge. Small labels uppercase 0.08em tracking, monospace-feel numbering ("01 —", "// STEP 02").
- Style: Swiss / brutalist-minimal: sharp corners, hairline borders, grid, lots of negative space, lime used sparingly for the one word that matters.
- Photo style (Higgsfield): real small-business interiors in Switzerland, natural light, muted tones, phone/laptop showing a clean website, no text inside images, no logos, no faces of identifiable real people.
- Format: carousel 1080×1350 (4:5); reels/stories 1080×1920 (9:16), keep text inside the central 4:5 safe zone.
