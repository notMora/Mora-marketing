# Hook & CTA library

Each hook = on-screen text (≤ 8 words) + spoken line + visual action in the first 1.5 s. Tag used in `content-log.jsonl` is in brackets; don't repeat the same tag two days running.

## Hook formulas
- [cost] "This is how much [missed DMs] cost your [salon] every month."
- [signs] "3 signs your website is sending clients to your competitor."
- [pov] "POV: it's 11pm and a new client wants to book your [barbershop]."
- [stop] "Stop answering the same 5 questions every day."
- [mistake] "The #1 mistake I see on [physio] websites in Switzerland."
- [redesign] "I redesigned a [beauty salon] website in 60 seconds (concept)."
- [vs] "Booking by DM vs booking in 3 taps."
- [secret] "Your competitor isn't better. Their website is just easier."
- [question] "Would a new client book you in under 30 seconds?"
- [test] "I tested [10] [tradespeople] websites on my phone. Here's what happened."
- [myth] "'I don't need a website, I have Instagram.' Let's talk."
- [number] "7 things every [hair salon] homepage needs (save this)."
- [story] "A client told me: 'I lose bookings every Sunday night.'" → only if true; else use "Owners tell me…"
- [price] "Why a cheap website ends up costing you clients."
- [reveal] Trend-audio reveal: chaos (DMs, calls, paper calendar) → calm (booking page, auto confirmations).
- [age] "Things I know after building websites for small businesses." (trend-adapted age/experience format)

## Retention devices (reels)
- Open loop: "…and the third one is the one nobody fixes."
- Pattern interrupt every 2–3 s (cut, zoom, text change).
- Loop ending: last frame/line connects back to the first.
- On-screen captions always (most watch muted). Big, centered, inside 4:5 safe zone.
- Length: trend reels 7–15 s; education/demo 25–45 s.

## Carousel devices
- Slide 1 = hook + promise ("swipe →" micro-label). Slide 2 = re-hook with the pain.
- Numbered slides "01/08" build completion. One idea per slide, ≤ 25 words.
- Penultimate slide = recap or before/after (most-saved slide). Last slide = CTA.

## CTA library (one per post)
Soft (TOFU):
- "Follow for one website fix a day for [niche] owners."
- "Send this to a [salon] owner who still books by DM."
Mid (MOFU):
- "Save this for your next website update."
- "Comment {KEYWORD} and I'll send you a free 3-minute review of your website."
Hard (BOFU):
- "Book a free 20-min call — link in bio. Design approved before you pay."
- "DM me {KEYWORD}. You only pay once you love the design."
- "2 project spots left this month" → **only if true** (ask Jordi); otherwise never use scarcity.

## Caption skeleton
Line 1: hook restated (≤ 125 chars, shows before "more").
Body: 3–6 short lines of value, line breaks, max 2 emojis.
CTA line with keyword.
Keywords for SEO in plain text (Instagram search): "[niche] website Switzerland", "online booking", "Zürich/Bern/Basel/Genève" when natural.
3–5 hashtags max, niche-specific (e.g. #physiotherapieschweiz #coiffeurzürich #smallbusinessswitzerland).
