# Content pillars

The daily slot (pillar, stage, niche, service, DM keyword) is computed by `node tools/plan.mjs` from `strategy/calendar.json`. This file explains what each pillar means. The weekly review may edit `calendar.json`, not this file.

## 1. MONEY LEAKS — TOFU
The owner's pain turned into lost money or time. Their current setup (no site, DM bookings, slow replies) costs them clients today.
- "Your salon gets DMs at 10pm. You answer at 9am. They already booked somewhere else."
- "3 signs your website is sending clients to your competitor."
- "Tradespeople: every missed call on a job site is a lost quote."
Angle: empathy first, then the cost, then a hint of the fix. No selling in the first 2 slides or 3 seconds.

## 2. CONCEPT REDESIGNS & DEMOS — MOFU
Show, don't tell: before/after concept redesigns (always labelled *concept*), a screen recording of a booking flow in 3 taps, the AI assistant answering at night, a 60-second "I'd rebuild this homepage like this" teardown.
- Generic/anonymised "before", never a real named business without permission.
- Tie each demo to one outcome: fewer messages, more bookings, faster replies.

## 3. OWNER PLAYBOOK — MOFU
Useful, save-worthy quick wins an owner can do today (proves expertise, earns saves/shares).
- Google Business Profile fixes, what goes above the fold, WhatsApp button, clear prices by service, photo checklist for tradespeople, "5 things a physio site must say to a new patient".
- End with: "Want this done for you?" + keyword.

## 4. OFFER & PROCESS — BOFU
Make hiring Jordi feel safe and easy.
- "How I work: 20-min call → you approve the design → then you pay."
- Objections: "I already have Instagram", "Websites are expensive", "I don't have time", "Wix is enough", "Will people really book online?"
- Always: risk reversal + direct CTA (call link / keyword).

## 5. BEHIND THE BUILD — TOFU/trust
The human behind the work: Jordi's workspace, details that show precision, building in public, lessons from building concept sites, day-in-the-life of a one-person studio. Builds the "know-like-trust" that closes calls.

## Rules
- One idea per post. One CTA per post.
- Rotate niche and service; never the same hook formula two days in a row (check `node tools/log.mjs recent`).
- Trend formats are containers: keep the format/audio, replace the story with the pillar message for SME owners.
